## Explanation

**Passive reporting structures** allow you to report what people think, believe, say, or claim **without naming a specific source**. They are essential in academic writing for presenting widely-held views, findings, or claims with appropriate distance and formality.

### Pattern 1 — "It + passive + that-clause"

```
It + is/was/has been + V3 + that + clause
```

The impersonal "it" is the dummy subject. The that-clause carries the actual content.

| Reporting verb | Example |
|---|---|
| said | **It is said that** the economy will recover. |
| believed | **It is believed that** climate change affects migration. |
| reported | **It was reported that** three people were injured. |
| claimed | **It is claimed that** the new drug is more effective. |
| thought | **It was thought that** the volcano had become dormant. |
| known | **It is known that** exercise reduces stress. |
| argued | **It is argued that** more funding is needed. |
| suggested | **It has been suggested that** the data may be flawed. |
| found | **It was found that** participants improved significantly. |
| shown | **It has been shown that** early intervention is effective. |
| estimated | **It is estimated that** 1 million species are at risk. |
| acknowledged | **It is acknowledged that** limitations exist. |
| assumed | **It was assumed that** the control group was representative. |

### Pattern 2 — "Subject + passive + to-infinitive"

```
Subject + is/was/has been + V3 + to-infinitive
```

The subject is the person or thing being reported about. More concise than Pattern 1.

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| is said to be | He **is said to be** the most influential economist of his generation. |
| is believed to have | The species **is believed to have** been extinct for 200 years. |
| is thought to be | This approach **is thought to be** the most effective. |
| is reported to have | The company **is reported to have** lost $2 billion. |
| is known to cause | Smoking **is known to cause** lung cancer. |
| is expected to | GDP **is expected to** grow by 3% next year. |
| is considered to be | She **is considered to be** a leading authority in the field. |
| is claimed to be | The product **is claimed to be** 100% natural. |

### Tense in Pattern 2 — present vs. perfect infinitive

| Infinitive | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| to + be/do | current state or future | He **is said to be** an expert. *(current)* |
| to + have + V3 | completed before now | The ruins **are believed to have been** built in 300 BC. |

> The company **is reported to have** lost millions. *(loss happened before "is reported")*
> The treatment **is thought to be** effective. *(current state)*

### Pattern 1 vs. Pattern 2 — converting between them

These two patterns express the same meaning:

| Pattern 1 | Pattern 2 |
|---|---|
| **It is said that** he is an expert. | He **is said to be** an expert. |
| **It is believed that** the ruins date to 300 BC. | The ruins **are believed to date** to 300 BC. |
| **It was reported that** the company lost money. | The company **was reported to have** lost money. |

### Common verbs for academic reporting

**Mental state verbs:** believe, think, know, understand, consider, assume, expect
**Communication verbs:** say, report, claim, argue, suggest, announce, state, note
**Discovery/evidence verbs:** find, show, demonstrate, prove, establish, confirm

## Bahasa Indonesia

### "Dikatakan bahwa" / "Dipercaya bahwa"

Indonesian uses **di- + kata kerja reporting + bahwa** for the same structure:

| Bahasa Indonesia | English Pattern 1 | English Pattern 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Dikatakan bahwa ekonomi akan pulih. | **It is said that** the economy will recover. | The economy **is said to** recover. |
| Dipercaya bahwa spesies ini sudah punah. | **It is believed that** this species is extinct. | This species **is believed to be** extinct. |
| Dilaporkan bahwa tiga orang terluka. | **It was reported that** three people were injured. | Three people **were reported to have been** injured. |

### Why Indonesian learners struggle with Pattern 2

Pattern 2 requires a grammatical transformation that doesn't exist in Indonesian. *Dia dikatakan seorang ahli* doesn't require an infinitive in Indonesian, but English does:
- ❌ He is said an expert.
- ✅ He **is said to be** an expert.

The "to be / to have" is the key element learners drop.

### Tense backshift in Pattern 1

When the reporting verb is past, the that-clause often needs past tense:
- *It **was** reported that three people **were** injured.* ✅
- *It **was** reported that three people **are** injured.* ❌ (unless still true now)

## Common Mistakes

**1. Dropping "to be" in Pattern 2**
- ❌ He is said an expert in the field.
- ✅ He **is said to be** an expert.

**2. Using "that" in Pattern 2 (Pattern 1 vs 2 confusion)**
- ❌ He is said that he is an expert.
- ✅ He **is said to be** an expert. *(Pattern 2 — no "that")*
- ✅ **It is said that** he is an expert. *(Pattern 1 — use "it" as subject)*

**3. Wrong infinitive tense — using "to be" when "to have" is needed**
- ❌ The company is reported to **lose** millions last year.
- ✅ The company is reported **to have lost** millions last year. *(past event → perfect infinitive)*

**4. Missing "it" as dummy subject in Pattern 1**
- ❌ Is said that the economy will recover.
- ✅ **It** is said that the economy will recover.

**5. Wrong tense of the reporting verb**
- ❌ It **is** reported that the earthquake killed 50 people in 1995.
- ✅ It **was** reported that the earthquake killed 50 people. *(past event → past tense)*

**6. Using active voice where passive reporting is expected in formal writing**
- ❌ People say that he is the best. *(too informal)*
- ✅ He **is said to be** the best. *(formal passive reporting)*

## Quick Summary

- **Pattern 1**: *It + is/was + V3 + that + clause* — "It is believed that..."
- **Pattern 2**: *Subject + is/was + V3 + to-infinitive* — "He is said to be..."
- Both patterns mean the same thing and can be converted to each other.
- Perfect infinitive (*to have + V3*): use when the reported action happened **before** the reporting verb.
- Common verbs: said, believed, reported, thought, known, found, shown, estimated, claimed, argued.
- ❌ "He is said that he is..." — Pattern 1 needs dummy "it"; Pattern 2 needs "to be".
- ❌ "The company is reported to lose" → ✅ "to have lost" for past events.
