## Explanation

**Reported speech** (also called indirect speech) uses noun clauses to report what someone said, thought, asked, or ordered — without quoting their exact words. Understanding this requires combining noun clause grammar (that-clauses and wh-clauses) with **tense backshift** and the right **reporting verb**.

### From direct to reported speech

| Direct speech | Reported speech |
|---|---|
| She said, "**I am** tired." | She said that she **was** tired. |
| He said, "**I will** call you." | He said that he **would** call me. |
| They said, "**We have** finished." | They said that they **had** finished. |

The verb in the noun clause shifts **one step back** in time.

### Tense backshift table

| Direct speech tense | Reported speech tense |
|---|---|
| Present simple (*am, work*) | Past simple (*was, worked*) |
| Present continuous (*is working*) | Past continuous (*was working*) |
| Past simple (*worked*) | Past perfect (*had worked*) |
| Present perfect (*has worked*) | Past perfect (*had worked*) |
| Will | Would |
| Can | Could |
| May | Might |
| Must | Had to |

> "I **work** here." → She said she **worked** there.
> "She **has finished**." → He reported that she **had finished**.
> "We **will** arrive soon." → They said they **would** arrive soon.

### When NOT to backshift

No backshift is needed when:
1. The reporting verb is **present tense**: *She says (that) she is tired.* *(current/ongoing)*
2. The reported fact is **still true**: *Galileo proved that the Earth **moves** around the sun.* *(still true)*
3. The time between reporting and the original speech is very short.

### Pronoun and time/place reference changes

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "I / we" | he / she / they |
| "you" | I / he / she / they (depends on context) |
| "here" | there |
| "now" | then |
| "today" | that day |
| "yesterday" | the day before / the previous day |
| "tomorrow" | the next day / the following day |
| "last week" | the week before / the previous week |
| "this" | that |

### Reporting statements — "say" and "tell"

```
say + (that) + clause
tell + person + (that) + clause
```

> "I'm leaving." → She **said** (that) she was leaving.
> "I'm leaving." → She **told me** (that) she was leaving.

⚠️ "Say" does NOT take a direct person object: ❌ *She said me that...*
⚠️ "Tell" REQUIRES a person object: ❌ *She told that...*

### Reporting questions — embedded question form

Reported questions use **statement word order** (no inversion, no auxiliary "do/does/did"):

**Wh-questions:**
```
She asked + wh-word + subject + verb
```
> "Where **do** you work?" → She asked me where **I worked**. *(not "where did I work")*
> "What **has** he done?" → He wanted to know what **she had done**.

**Yes/no questions:**
```
She asked + if/whether + subject + verb
```
> "**Are** you free tomorrow?" → She asked **if I was** free the next day.
> "**Have** you finished?" → He wanted to know **whether I had** finished.

### Reporting orders and requests

| Type | Verb | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Orders | tell, order, instruct | tell + person + **to-infinitive** |
| Requests | ask | ask + person + **to-infinitive** |
| Advice | advise | advise + person + **to-infinitive** |

> "**Close** the door." → He **told** her **to close** the door.
> "**Please help** me." → She **asked** him **to help** her.
> "**Don't be** late." → She **told** them **not to be** late.

### Key reporting verbs beyond "say" and "tell"

| Verb | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| claim | claim + that | She **claimed that** she had never been there. |
| deny | deny + -ing / that | He **denied stealing** the money. / He **denied that** he had stolen it. |
| admit | admit + -ing / that | She **admitted making** a mistake. |
| suggest | suggest + -ing / that | He **suggested waiting** / He **suggested that** we wait. |
| promise | promise + to-inf / that | She **promised to return** / She **promised that** she would return. |
| warn | warn + person + not to | They **warned us not to** swim there. |
| explain | explain + that | She **explained that** the delay was unavoidable. |
| confirm | confirm + that | He **confirmed that** the meeting was cancelled. |
| announce | announce + that | The company **announced that** it was expanding. |
| argue | argue + that | The researcher **argued that** the data was misleading. |

## Bahasa Indonesia

### No tense backshift in Indonesian

Bahasa Indonesia **does not backshift tenses** in reported speech — the verb stays the same regardless of the reporting verb tense. This is the biggest source of reported speech errors for Indonesian learners.

| Bahasa Indonesia | Wrong English | Correct English |
|---|---|---|
| Dia bilang dia **lelah**. | She said she **is** tired. ❌ | She said she **was** tired. ✅ |
| Dia berkata bahwa dia **akan pergi**. | He said he **will** go. ❌ | He said he **would** go. ✅ |
| Mereka mengumumkan bahwa proyek itu **selesai**. | They announced the project **is** complete. ❌ | They announced the project **was** complete. ✅ |

### "Katanya" / "Dia bilang" — reporting without backshift

Indonesian often reports speech with *katanya* (reportedly / he said) or *dia bilang* (she said) with no structural change. English requires the full reporting structure with backshift.

### Reporting questions — word order trap

Just like embedded questions from the previous module, reported questions must use statement order:

| Bahasa Indonesia | Wrong English | Correct English |
|---|---|---|
| Dia bertanya di mana saya bekerja. | She asked where **do I work**. ❌ | She asked where **I worked**. ✅ |
| Dia ingin tahu apakah saya sudah selesai. | He wanted to know **have I finished**. ❌ | He wanted to know **if I had finished**. ✅ |

## Common Mistakes

**1. No tense backshift**
- ❌ She said that she **is** busy.
- ✅ She said that she **was** busy.

**2. Question word order in reported questions**
- ❌ He asked where **does she live**.
- ✅ He asked where **she lived**.

**3. Using "say" with a person object**
- ❌ She **said me** that she was leaving.
- ✅ She **told me** that she was leaving.

**4. Using "deny" with a that-clause using "not"**
- ❌ He denied **that he didn't take** the money.
- ✅ He denied **that he took** the money. / He denied **taking** the money.

  *"Deny" already implies the negative — don't add "not".*

**5. "Suggest" + should (Indonesian literal translation)**
- ❌ He suggested that we **should to** wait.
- ✅ He suggested that we **wait**. *(subjunctive)*
- ✅ He suggested **waiting**. *(-ing form)*

**6. Forgetting pronoun changes**
- Direct: "I will call **you** tomorrow."
- ❌ She said she would call **you** the next day.
- ✅ She said she would call **me** the next day. *(if you are the listener)*

**7. Not changing time expressions**
- ❌ She said she would arrive **tomorrow**.
- ✅ She said she would arrive **the next day / the following day**.

## Quick Summary

- Reported speech = direct quote → noun clause with **tense backshift**.
- Backshift: present → past | will → would | can → could | may → might | must → had to.
- **"Say" + (that)** — no person object. **"Tell" + person + (that)** — person required.
- Reported **wh-questions**: wh-word + **statement order** (not question order).
- Reported **yes/no questions**: if/whether + **statement order**.
- Reported **orders/requests**: tell/ask + person + **to-infinitive**. Negative: **not to** + infinitive.
- Key verbs: claim that, deny -ing/that, admit -ing/that, suggest -ing/that, promise that, warn not to, explain that.
- No backshift needed if: reporting verb is present tense, or the fact is still currently true.
