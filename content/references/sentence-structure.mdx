## English Sentence Structure Formulas

Memorize these formulas. They cover **~80% of the grammar tested** on TOEFL and IELTS.

### Notation

- **S** = subject (I, you, he, she, it, we, they, or a noun)
- **V1** = base verb (work, eat, go)
- **V2** = past simple (worked, ate, went)
- **V3** = past participle (worked, eaten, gone)
- **Ving** = verb + -ing (working, eating, going)
- **O** = object / rest of sentence

### Main Explanation

#### 1. Present Simple

For habits, facts, and routines.

| Form | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + V1 (+ -s for he/she/it) + O | She **works** here. They **work** here. |
| Negative | S + do/does + not + V1 + O | She **does not work** here. |
| Question | Do/Does + S + V1 + O ? | **Does** she **work** here? |

⚠️ Add **-s** to the verb only for he / she / it. Easy to forget.

> Time markers: every day, always, usually, often, sometimes, never

#### 2. Present Continuous

For actions happening **right now** or around the present.

| Form | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + am/is/are + Ving + O | She **is working** now. |
| Negative | S + am/is/are + not + Ving + O | She **is not working**. |
| Question | Am/Is/Are + S + Ving + O ? | **Is** she **working**? |

> Time markers: now, right now, at the moment, currently, today, this week

#### 3. Past Simple

For completed actions in the past.

| Form | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + V2 + O | She **worked** yesterday. |
| Negative | S + did + not + V1 + O | She **didn't work** yesterday. |
| Question | Did + S + V1 + O ? | **Did** she **work** yesterday? |

⚠️ For *to be*: use **was/were** directly — no "did".

> Time markers: yesterday, last week, two days ago, in 2010, when I was a child

#### 4. Present Perfect

For actions connecting the past to now (no specific past time).

| Form | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + have/has + V3 + O | She **has worked** here for 5 years. |
| Negative | S + have/has + not + V3 + O | She **hasn't worked** today. |
| Question | Have/Has + S + V3 + O ? | **Has** she **worked** here long? |

⚠️ **Has** with he / she / it; **have** with I / you / we / they.

> Time markers: for, since, already, yet, just, ever, never, recently, lately, so far

#### 5. Future — "will" vs "going to"

##### "will" — for decisions, predictions, and offers

| Form | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + will + V1 + O | I **will help** you. |
| Negative | S + will + not + V1 + O | I **won't help** him. |
| Question | Will + S + V1 + O ? | **Will** you **help** me? |

##### "going to" — for **plans** and **clear evidence**

| Form | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + am/is/are + going to + V1 + O | She **is going to study** abroad. |
| Negative | S + am/is/are + not + going to + V1 + O | She **isn't going to study**. |
| Question | Am/Is/Are + S + going to + V1 + O ? | **Are** they **going to leave**? |

##### When to use which

| Use | Tense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Spontaneous decision | will | "The phone is ringing." → "I'**ll get** it." |
| Prediction (no evidence) | will | I think it **will rain** tomorrow. |
| Prediction (clear evidence) | going to | Look at those clouds — it'**s going to rain**. |
| Plan / intention | going to | I**'m going to visit** my parents next week. |
| Promise / offer | will | I **'ll always love** you. |

### Side-by-side: how each tense looks

| Tense | She + study (positive) |
|---|---|
| Present simple | She **studies** every day. |
| Present continuous | She **is studying** now. |
| Past simple | She **studied** yesterday. |
| Present perfect | She **has studied** for 3 hours. |
| Future (will) | She **will study** tomorrow. |
| Future (going to) | She **is going to study** tomorrow. |

### English vs Bahasa Indonesia

In Bahasa Indonesia, the verb **never changes** for tense. Time is shown by
context words alone:

| Indonesian | English (verb changes) |
|---|---|
| Saya **belajar** sekarang. | I **am studying** now. |
| Saya **belajar** kemarin. | I **studied** yesterday. |
| Saya **sudah belajar**. | I **have studied**. |
| Saya **akan belajar** besok. | I **will study** tomorrow. |

The Indonesian verb *belajar* stays the same in all four sentences. In
English, every tense uses a **different verb form and structure**.

### Common Mistakes by Indonesian Learners

- ❌ She **work** here. → ✅ She **works** here. *(third person -s)*
- ❌ I **am study** English. → ✅ I **am studying** English. *(continuous needs -ing)*
- ❌ He **didn't went** home. → ✅ He **didn't go** home. *(after "didn't" use V1)*
- ❌ I **am know** him. → ✅ I **know** him. *(don't use "to be" with action verbs)*
- ❌ She **have** finished. → ✅ She **has** finished. *(use "has" for he/she/it)*
- ❌ I **have ate** breakfast. → ✅ I **have eaten** breakfast. *(perfect needs V3)*
- ❌ I **will to call** you. → ✅ I **will call** you. *(no "to" after "will")*
- ❌ Tomorrow I **go** to Jakarta. → ✅ Tomorrow I **will go** / I **'m going to go** to Jakarta.

### Quick Summary

- **Present Simple:** S + V1 (-s for he/she/it). For routines.
- **Present Continuous:** S + am/is/are + Ving. For now.
- **Past Simple:** S + V2 (or did + V1 for negatives/questions). For finished past.
- **Present Perfect:** S + have/has + V3. For past-to-now.
- **Future:** *will* (decisions, predictions) or *going to* (plans).
- After **did/didn't** or **will/can/must/should** → use V1.
- After **have/has** → use V3.
- After **am/is/are/was/were** → use Ving (continuous) or V3 (passive).
