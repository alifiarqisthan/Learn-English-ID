## Top 100 Irregular Verbs

If you only memorize one list in English, make it this one. **Irregular verbs
are the most common verbs**, and you can't form past tenses, present perfect,
or passive voice without them.

This list groups verbs by **pattern**, which is much faster than rote memorization.

### Memorization tips

1. **Learn by pattern, not alphabetically.** Verbs that rhyme often follow the same change (sing/sang, ring/rang, swim/swam).
2. **Master the top 10 first.** *be, have, do, go, get, see, take, make, come, know* — these alone cover ~30% of all verb usage.
3. **Use the verb in a full sentence.** "I sang" sticks better than "sing → sang".
4. **Color-code your weak ones.** Highlight the verbs you keep getting wrong and review them daily.
5. **Practice V3 with "have."** Say "have eaten, have written, have gone" out loud to build the V3 reflex.

### Pattern 6 — Totally irregular (memorize first)

These three are the most-used verbs in English. Master them before any others.

| V1 | V2 | V3 | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **be** | **was / were** | **been** | Two past forms by subject |
| **go** | **went** | **gone** | "went" comes from a different verb (*wend*) |
| **do** | **did** | **done** | Used as auxiliary (do you...?) and main verb |

### Pattern 7 — Special / alternates

| V1 | V2 | V3 | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| become | became | become | V1 = V3 |
| come | came | come | V1 = V3 |
| get | got | gotten *(US)* / got *(UK)* | varies by region |
| have | had | had | also auxiliary |
| lie /lai/ (recline) | lay | lain | confused with *lay* (put down) |
| lay (put down) | laid | laid | takes a direct object |
| shine | shone *(light)* / shined *(polished)* | shone / shined | meaning-dependent |

### Common Mistakes by Indonesian Learners

- ❌ He **goed** home. → ✅ He **went** home.
- ❌ She has **wrote** a letter. → ✅ She has **written** a letter.
- ❌ I have **ate** breakfast. → ✅ I have **eaten** breakfast.
- ❌ Did you **broke** the glass? → ✅ Did you **break** the glass? *(V1 after "did")*
- ❌ The book was **steal**. → ✅ The book was **stolen**.
- ❌ I have **saw** that movie. → ✅ I have **seen** that movie.
- ❌ He **bringed** flowers. → ✅ He **brought** flowers.

### Quick Summary

- ~200 irregular verbs exist; the **top 100** cover almost all real usage
- Group by pattern: **no change**, **V2 = V3**, **vowel change**, **-en/-n V3**, **totally irregular**
- Master *be, have, do, go, get, see, take, make, come, know* first
- After *did/didn't* → V1; after *have/has/had* → V3; passive voice → V3
- This list is one of the highest-ROI things to memorize for TOEFL/IELTS
