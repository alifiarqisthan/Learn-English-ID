## Prepositions Quick Reference

Prepositions are tiny words that English speakers pick **by feel** — but
Indonesian learners can't rely on feel. The good news: TOEFL/IELTS test the
**same combinations** over and over. Memorize this list and you'll cover 90%
of what you need.

### Main Explanation

#### Prepositions of time — in / on / at

| Preposition | Use for | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **in** | Long periods (months, years, centuries, parts of day) | **in** May, **in** 2026, **in** the 19th century, **in** the morning |
| **on** | Specific days and dates | **on** Monday, **on** July 4th, **on** my birthday, **on** the weekend (US) |
| **at** | Specific times and night | **at** 5pm, **at** noon, **at** midnight, **at** night, **at** the moment |

Memory trick: **in** = inside a long stretch of time; **on** = on a specific day; **at** = at a precise point.

> She was born **in** 1995, **on** March 5th, **at** 7am.

##### Other time prepositions

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| during | within a period | **during** the meeting |
| for | length of time | **for** two hours |
| since | starting point | **since** 2010 |
| by | not later than | by Friday |
| until / till | up to a time | until tomorrow |
| from … to … | range | from Monday to Friday |
| ago | time before now | two days **ago** |

#### Prepositions of place — in / on / at

| Preposition | Use for | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **in** | Inside a 3D space, cities, countries | **in** the room, **in** Jakarta, **in** Indonesia, **in** the car |
| **on** | On a surface, streets, floors | **on** the table, **on** Jalan Sudirman, **on** the second floor |
| **at** | A specific point or address | **at** the door, **at** the bus stop, **at** Jl. Sudirman 10 |

> She lives **in** Jakarta, **on** Jl. Sudirman, **at** number 10.

##### Other place prepositions

| Preposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| above / below | higher / lower than |
| over / under | directly above / below |
| in front of / behind | facing / opposite |
| next to / beside | adjacent to |
| between | among two things |
| among | among more than two |
| near / far from | close to / distant from |
| inside / outside | within / out of |
| across (from) | on the opposite side |

| Verb + preposition | Example |
|---|---|

| belong **to** | This book belongs **to** her. |

| consist **of** | Water consists **of** hydrogen and oxygen. |
| depend **on** | It depends **on** the weather. |
| dream **of / about** | She dreams **of** becoming a doctor. |
| listen **to** | I listen **to** music every day. |
| look **at / for / after** | Look **at** this. I'm looking **for** my keys. She looks **after** the kids. |

### English vs Bahasa Indonesia

Indonesian uses one preposition where English uses several different ones:

| Indonesian | English options |
|---|---|
| **di** (in/on/at) | in, on, at, inside, outside |
| **ke** (to) | to, into, onto, towards |
| **dari** (from) | from, of, since, off |
| **dengan** (with) | with, by, using |
| **untuk** (for) | for, to (in order to) |
| **pada** (on, at — formal) | on, at, in (time) |

Because **one Indonesian word maps to many English ones**, learners often pick the wrong English preposition. The fix is to **memorize whole verb-preposition combinations** as single units.

### Common Mistakes by Indonesian Learners

- ❌ I'm good **in** English. → ✅ I'm good **at** English.
- ❌ I'm interested **on** music. → ✅ I'm interested **in** music.
- ❌ He arrived **to** Jakarta. → ✅ He arrived **in** Jakarta.
- ❌ I'm waiting my friend. → ✅ I'm waiting **for** my friend.
- ❌ I listen music. → ✅ I listen **to** music.
- ❌ Discuss **about** the problem. → ✅ Discuss the problem. *(no preposition)*
- ❌ Married **with** John. → ✅ Married **to** John.
- ❌ Depend **of** the weather. → ✅ Depend **on** the weather.
- ❌ **In** Monday. → ✅ **On** Monday.
- ❌ **On** the morning. → ✅ **In** the morning.
- ❌ Different **with** that one. → ✅ Different **from** that one.

### Quick Summary

- **Time:** *in* (months/years/parts of day), *on* (days/dates), *at* (precise times)
- **Place:** *in* (cities/3D spaces), *on* (surfaces/streets), *at* (specific points/addresses)
- **Adjective + preposition:** memorize as fixed pairs (good **at**, interested **in**, married **to**)
- **Verb + preposition:** memorize as fixed pairs (listen **to**, wait **for**, depend **on**)
- "Discuss" and "marry" do **not** take "about"/"with" in English — they're directly transitive
