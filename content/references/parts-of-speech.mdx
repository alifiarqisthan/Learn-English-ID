## Parts of Speech Cheat Sheet

Every English word belongs to one of **8 categories**. Knowing which category a
word is tells you what role it can play in a sentence — and many TOEFL/IELTS
error-identification questions test exactly this.

### Main Explanation

| # | Part of Speech | Indonesian | What it does | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Noun** | *kata benda* | Names a person, place, thing, or idea | book, Jakarta, teacher, freedom |
| 2 | **Verb** | *kata kerja* | Action or state of being | run, eat, be, seem, become |
| 3 | **Adjective** | *kata sifat* | Describes a noun | tall, red, smart, Indonesian |
| 4 | **Adverb** | *kata keterangan* | Modifies a verb, adjective, or other adverb | quickly, very, well, often |
| 5 | **Pronoun** | *kata ganti* | Replaces a noun | I, you, he, she, it, they, who |
| 6 | **Preposition** | *kata depan* | Shows relationship (time, place, direction) | in, on, at, with, from, between |
| 7 | **Conjunction** | *kata penghubung* | Joins words, phrases, or clauses | and, but, or, because, although |
| 8 | **Interjection** | *kata seru* | Expresses emotion | wow, oh, ouch, hey |

### Each in detail

#### 1. Noun (kata benda)

- **Common nouns:** book, car, city
- **Proper nouns:** Jakarta, Anita, Monas
- **Abstract nouns:** love, freedom, happiness
- **Countable / uncountable:** *one apple, two apples* (countable) vs *water, rice, information* (uncountable)

> **The teacher** gave **the students** a **book**. *(3 nouns)*

#### 2. Verb (kata kerja)

- **Action verbs:** run, write, eat, study
- **Linking verbs:** be (am/is/are/was/were), seem, become, feel
- **Auxiliary (helping) verbs:** do, have, will, can, must

> She **wrote** a letter. He **is** tired. They **have finished**.

#### 3. Adjective (kata sifat)

Describes a noun. In English, adjectives come **before** the noun (opposite of Indonesian!).

> A **red** car. The **tall** building. An **interesting** book.

| English | Indonesian |
|---|---|
| a **red** car | mobil **merah** |
| an **interesting** book | buku **yang menarik** |

#### 4. Adverb (kata keterangan)

Most adverbs end in **-ly** (quick → quickly). They tell you *how, when, where, how often, how much*.

> She runs **quickly**. He arrived **yesterday**. They study **here**. We **often** meet.

#### 5. Pronoun (kata ganti)

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Subject | I, you, he, she, it, we, they |
| Object | me, you, him, her, it, us, them |
| Possessive | my, your, his, her, its, our, their |
| Demonstrative | this, that, these, those |
| Interrogative | who, what, which, whose |

→ See [Subject, Object, Possessive Pronouns →](/references/pronouns) for the full table.

#### 6. Preposition (kata depan)

Small words that link nouns to other parts of the sentence.

> The book is **on** the table. I'll see you **at** 5pm. She's **from** Bandung.

→ See [Prepositions Quick Reference →](/references/prepositions).

#### 7. Conjunction (kata penghubung)

| Type | Examples | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Coordinating (FANBOYS) | for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so | Join equal parts |
| Subordinating | because, although, if, when, while | Introduce dependent clauses |

> I was tired **but** I finished. We stayed home **because** it rained.

#### 8. Interjection (kata seru)

> **Wow!** That was amazing. **Oh**, I forgot. **Hey!** Wait for me.

### English vs Bahasa Indonesia

| Feature | English | Indonesian |
|---|---|---|
| Adjective position | **before** noun: *red car* | **after** noun: *mobil merah* |
| Adverb formation | usually *adjective + -ly* | usually *dengan + adjective* (*dengan cepat*) |
| Articles | a / an / the (required) | none — context is enough |
| Plural marking | add -s to nouns | reduplicate or use *banyak* (*buku-buku*, *banyak buku*) |
| Subject/object distinction | "I" (subject) vs "me" (object) | same word: *saya* / *aku* |

### Common Mistakes by Indonesian Learners

- ❌ **Car red** is fast. → ✅ The **red car** is fast. *(adjective before noun)*
- ❌ I run **with quick**. → ✅ I run **quickly**. *(use adverb form, not preposition + adjective)*
- ❌ I have **two book**. → ✅ I have **two books**. *(plural -s on countable nouns)*
- ❌ **He** book is here. → ✅ **His** book is here. *(possessive, not subject pronoun)*
- ❌ I go **to** home. → ✅ I go **home**. *("home" doesn't take "to" as adverb)*
- ❌ She is **interest** in music. → ✅ She is **interested** in music. *(adjective form)*

### Quick Summary

- 8 categories: **noun, verb, adjective, adverb, pronoun, preposition, conjunction, interjection**
- Adjective goes **before** the noun in English (the opposite of Indonesian)
- Most adverbs end in **-ly**
- Pronouns change form depending on subject vs object role
- Articles **a / an / the** are required in English even though Indonesian has no equivalent
