## Welcome to Reported Speech

Reported (indirect) speech changes the form of what someone said. Key changes: tense backshift, pronouns, and time/place expressions.

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "I **am** tired," she said. | She said she **was** tired. |
| "I **will** call you," he said. | He said he **would** call me. |
| "**Have** you finished?" she asked. | She asked if I **had** finished. |
| "**Close** the door," he said. | He told me **to close** the door. |
