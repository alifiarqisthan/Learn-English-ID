## Welcome to the Future Tenses

English has **four future tenses**. They cover everything from a snap decision
("I'll get it!") to a long-running plan ("By 2030, I will have been working
here for 10 years"):

| Tense | Angle | Quick example |
|---|---|---|
| **Future Simple** | Predictions, decisions, promises | I **will help** you. / I'**m going to study** abroad. |
| **Future Continuous** | Action in progress at a future moment | This time tomorrow I **will be flying** to Bali. |
| **Future Perfect** | Action completed by a future point | By 2030, I **will have graduated**. |
| **Future Perfect Continuous** | Duration of an action by a future point | By December, I **will have been studying** English for 5 years. |

### Why this matters for Indonesian learners

Bahasa Indonesia uses **one or two markers** for the future: *akan* (will),
*mau* (going to/want to), and *nanti* (later). The verb itself never changes.

In English, you need to:
- Pick *will* or *going to* for simple future (different uses!)
- Form *will be + Ving* for ongoing future actions
- Form *will have + V3* for completed-by-deadline reasoning
