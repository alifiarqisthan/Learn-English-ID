## Welcome to Subject-Verb Agreement

The verb must agree with its subject in number. This is a frequent error for Indonesian learners because Bahasa Indonesia has no verb conjugation for number.

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Singular subject → singular verb | The team **is** ready. |
| Plural subject → plural verb | The students **are** ready. |
| Indefinite pronoun → singular | **Everyone has** a ticket. |
| Intervening phrase — ignore it | The box of chocolates **was** opened. |
| Either…or / neither…nor → nearest subject | Neither the teacher nor **the students were** prepared. |
