## Welcome to Zero & First Conditionals

These conditionals describe real or likely situations.

| Type | Form | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zero** | If + present, present | General truth | If you **heat** ice, it **melts**. |
| **First** | If + present, will + V1 | Real future possibility | If it **rains**, I **will stay** home. |
