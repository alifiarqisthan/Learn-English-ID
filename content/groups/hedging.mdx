## Welcome to Hedging Language

**Hedging** means expressing claims with appropriate caution — avoiding absolute statements. It is essential for IELTS Academic Writing band 7+ and TOEFL integrated tasks.

| Device | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Modal verbs** | *may, might, could, would* |
| **Adverbs** | *possibly, probably, generally, typically, largely* |
| **Verbs** | *seem, appear, tend to, suggest, indicate* |
| **Phrases** | *it is possible that, it appears that, to some extent* |
| **Approximators** | *approximately, around, about, roughly* |
