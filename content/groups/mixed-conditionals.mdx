## Welcome to Mixed Conditionals & Alternatives

Mixed conditionals combine a past condition with a present result, or vice versa. Also covers conditional connectors beyond *if*.

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| Past condition → present result | If I **had taken** that job, I **would be** in London now. |
| Present condition → past result | If she **weren't** shy, she **would have applied**. |
| **Unless** | **Unless** you study, you won't pass. |
| **Provided that / as long as** | You may attend **provided that** you register. |
