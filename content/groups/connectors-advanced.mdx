## Welcome to Advanced Cohesive Devices

Beyond basic connectors, academic English uses subtler cohesive tools that link ideas across sentences and paragraphs.

| Device | Type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **this / these / such** | Reference | Prices rose sharply. **This** trend worried economists. |
| **the former / the latter** | Reference | Speed and accuracy are both important; **the latter** is harder to measure. |
| **do so / does so** | Substitution | She studied hard, and her classmates **did so** too. |
| **not only…but also** | Correlative | **Not only** did she pass, **but** she scored top marks. |
| **by contrast / notwithstanding** | Formal contrast | Exports grew; **by contrast**, imports fell. |
