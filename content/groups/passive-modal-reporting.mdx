## Welcome to Passive with Modals & Reporting

Passive with modals and reporting verbs is essential for academic English — it allows you to express necessity, possibility, and reported information without naming an agent.

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| **Modal passive** | This form **must be completed**. Results **should be verified**. |
| **It is + V3 + that-clause** | **It is believed that** prices will rise. |
| **Subject + is + V3 + to-infinitive** | He **is said to be** a leading expert. |
| **Passive + perfect** | The data **had been collected** before analysis began. |
