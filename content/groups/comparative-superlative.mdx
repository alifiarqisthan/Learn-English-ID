## Welcome to Comparatives & Superlatives

| Structure | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Comparative** (2 things) | -er / more + adj + than | She is **taller** / **more confident than** him. |
| **Superlative** (3+ things) | the -est / most + adj | This is **the tallest** / **most complex** building. |
| **Comparative adverb** | more + adv + than | She works **more efficiently than** before. |
| **Superlative adverb** | the most + adv | He runs **the fastest** on the team. |

The -er/-est vs more/most choice depends on syllable count and word class.
