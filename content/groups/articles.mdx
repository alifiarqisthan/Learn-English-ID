## Welcome to Articles

Indonesian has no articles — *kucing* means "a cat," "the cat," or "cats" depending entirely on context. This is why articles are the single most common grammatical error Indonesian learners make in IELTS Writing.

| Article | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **a / an** | First mention, one of many | I saw **a** cat. She is **an** engineer. |
| **the** | Specific, known to both parties | **The** cat was black. Pass me **the** salt. |
| **∅** | General plural or uncountable | **Cats** are independent. I love **music**. |
