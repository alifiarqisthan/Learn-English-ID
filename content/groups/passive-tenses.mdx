## Welcome to Passive Voice — Tenses

The passive shifts focus from the doer to the action or receiver. Form: **to be (conjugated) + V3**.

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | They make cars here. | Cars **are made** here. |
| Past Simple | Someone stole my wallet. | My wallet **was stolen**. |
| Present Perfect | They have fixed the road. | The road **has been fixed**. |
| Future Simple | They will announce the results. | The results **will be announced**. |
| Present Continuous | They are reviewing the proposal. | The proposal **is being reviewed**. |
