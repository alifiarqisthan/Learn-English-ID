## Welcome to Quantifiers & Determiners

Quantifiers specify how much or how many. The choice depends on whether the noun is countable or uncountable, and the positive, negative, or interrogative context.

| Quantifier | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **many / few** | Countable nouns | **Many** students passed. **Few** errors were found. |
| **much / little** | Uncountable nouns | **Much** time was wasted. **Little** evidence exists. |
| **a lot of** | Both | **A lot of** people / water. |
| **each / every** | Singular countable (universal) | **Each** student. **Every** answer. |
| **both / all** | Multiple | **Both** options. **All** students. |
| **either / neither** | Two options | **Either** answer is correct. |
