## Welcome to Adverb Clauses

Adverb clauses modify the main verb or sentence, answering when, why, how, or under what condition.

| Type | Conjunctions | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Time** | when, while, before, after, until | **Before she left**, she locked the door. |
| **Contrast** | although, whereas, even though | **Although it rained**, we went out. |
| **Cause** | because, since, as | **Because he studied**, he passed. |
| **Result** | so…that, such…that | It was **so hot that** we stayed inside. |
| **Purpose** | so that, in order to | She studies **so that** she can graduate. |
