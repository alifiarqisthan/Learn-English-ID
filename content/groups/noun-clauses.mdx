## Welcome to Noun Clauses

A noun clause functions as a noun inside a larger sentence. Word order changes when a question becomes embedded — one of the most tested TOEFL structures.

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **that-clause** | I know **that she is right**. |
| **wh-clause** | I don't know **where he went**. |
| **if/whether clause** | Can you tell me **if the store is open**? |
