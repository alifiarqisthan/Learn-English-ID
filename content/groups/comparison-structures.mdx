## Welcome to As…As and Double Comparatives

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| **as + adj + as** (equality) | He runs **as fast as** his brother. |
| **not as + adj + as** (inequality) | This exam is **not as hard as** I expected. |
| **twice / three times as … as** | She earns **twice as much as** I do. |
| **Double comparative** | **The more** you practice, **the better** you get. |
| **Comparative + and + comparative** | It is getting **hotter and hotter**. |
