## Welcome to Basic Connectors

Connectors show the logical relationship between ideas. Getting them right is one of the fastest ways to improve IELTS Writing Coherence & Cohesion scores.

| Relationship | Connectors |
|---|---|
| **Addition** | and, also, in addition, furthermore, moreover |
| **Contrast** | but, however, on the other hand, nevertheless, yet |
| **Cause** | because, since, as, due to, owing to |
| **Result** | so, therefore, consequently, as a result, thus |
| **Concession** | although, even though, despite, in spite of |
| **Example** | for example, for instance, such as, namely |
