## Welcome to Modal Perfects & Deduction

Modal perfects combine a modal with **have + V3** to reason about the past — what was possible, necessary, or regrettable.

| Structure | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **must have + V3** | Certain past deduction | She **must have left** already. |
| **can't have + V3** | Impossible past deduction | He **can't have known** — he just arrived. |
| **could have + V3** | Past possibility (not taken) | I **could have helped** but I didn't. |
| **should have + V3** | Past regret / criticism | You **should have called** me. |
| **would have + V3** | Conditional past result | I **would have come** if invited. |
