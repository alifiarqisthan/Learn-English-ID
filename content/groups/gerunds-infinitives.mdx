## Welcome to Gerunds & Infinitives

After most verbs you must choose between the gerund (-ing) and the infinitive (to + V1). Some verbs take only one; others take both with a change in meaning.

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| Verb + gerund | I enjoy **reading**. She avoids **making** mistakes. |
| Verb + infinitive | She wants **to travel**. He decided **to stay**. |
| Both — same meaning | He started **reading** / **to read**. |
| Both — different meaning | I stopped **smoking**. / I stopped **to smoke**. |
| Gerund as subject | **Swimming** is great exercise. |
| After preposition | She is good **at explaining** things. |
