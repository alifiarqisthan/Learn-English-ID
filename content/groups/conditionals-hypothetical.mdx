## Welcome to Second & Third Conditionals

These conditionals describe hypothetical or impossible situations.

| Type | Form | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Second** | If + past, would + V1 | Hypothetical present/future | If I **had** more time, I **would travel**. |
| **Third** | If + past perfect, would have + V3 | Impossible past | If I **had studied**, I **would have passed**. |
