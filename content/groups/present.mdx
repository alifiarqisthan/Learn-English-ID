## Welcome to the Present Tenses

The "present" in English is **not one tense — it's four**. Each one shows the
present from a different angle:

| Tense | Angle | Quick example |
|---|---|---|
| **Present Simple** | Habits, facts, permanent states | I **work** at a hospital. |
| **Present Continuous** | Action in progress now | I **am writing** an email. |
| **Present Perfect** | Past that connects to now | I **have lived** here for 5 years. |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | Duration of an ongoing action up to now | I **have been studying** for 2 hours. |

### Why this matters for Indonesian learners

In Bahasa Indonesia, **the verb does not change for tense**. The same word
*kerja* covers "work," "am working," "have worked," and "have been working" —
context words like *sedang*, *sudah*, *selama* do all the work.

In English, you don't get to skip the verb form. Picking the right one of
these four tenses is the **#1 grammar challenge** for Indonesian learners
preparing for TOEFL/IELTS.

This group will teach you the four present tenses one by one, then a final
**Group Test** ties them all together with contrast questions — so you don't
just know each tense, you know **when to choose which**.
