## Welcome to Relative Clauses

A relative clause modifies a noun. Choosing the right relative pronoun and understanding the defining vs non-defining distinction are both tested heavily on TOEFL and IELTS.

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Defining** | The woman **who called you** is outside. |
| **Non-defining** | My brother, **who lives in London**, is a doctor. |
| **Reduced** | The man **standing by the door** is the manager. |
