## Welcome to Sentence Types & Basic Structure

Every English sentence has a subject and a predicate. Understanding the four sentence types is the foundation for writing clear, varied academic prose.

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | One independent clause | She studies every night. |
| **Compound** | Two independent clauses + coordinator | She studies, and he reads. |
| **Complex** | Independent + dependent clause | She studies because she wants to pass. |
| **Compound-complex** | Two independent + one dependent | She studies, and he reads while music plays. |
